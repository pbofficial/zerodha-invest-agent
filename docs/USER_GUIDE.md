# 📖 Zerodha Invest Agent: User Guide

Welcome to your Intelligent Investment Agent. This guide explains how to navigate the dashboard, understand AI-driven insights, and execute trades safely.

## 🔐 1. Authentication & Login
When you launch the application, you'll be greeted by the **Secure Gateway**.
- **Login with Zerodha**: Click the button to be redirected to Zerodha Kite.
- **PIN/2FA**: Complete your login on the Zerodha site.
- **Redirection**: You will be automatically sent back to your private dashboard.

---

## 📈 2. Portfolio View
The primary tab for monitoring your current financial health.
- **Metrics Bar**: Track Total Invested, Current Value, and Unrealized P&L.
- **Risk Profile**: An AI-calculated status (Conservative vs. High Risk) based on your holdings.
- **Portfolio Holdings**: A detailed list of your stocks, their cost basis, and real-time performance.
- **Distribution Charts**: Visualize your exposure by Sector and Market Cap.

---

## 🎯 3. Trading Desk (Core Workflow)
This is where the magic happens. Use the Trading Desk to rebalance your portfolio.

### Step 1: Run Analysis 🤖
Click **"Run Analysis"** to trigger the Gemini AI. The agent will:
1. Scan global news for your universe.
2. Evaluate financial health.
3. Check market status.
4. Propose buy/sell orders based on your budget.

### Step 2: Review Logic 🧠
- **Logic Stream**: Read the "Why" behind every suggestion in the right-hand panel.
- **Signals**: 
    - 🌟 **STRONG BUY**: High conviction based on positive momentum/news.
    - ✅ **BUY**: Standard allocation.
    - ➕ **ADD**: Accumulate more of a winning position.
    - ⏸️ **HOLD**: No action required.
    - ⚠️ **SELL**: AI suggests exiting or trimming.

### Step 3: Manual Overrides ✍️
- **🛒 Buy Qty**: You can manually edit the "Buy Qty" column in the workspace. The **Estimated Cost** will update in real-time.
- **Freedom**: Use the AI suggestions as a guide, but you have the final word on every single share.

### Step 4: Submit & Approve 📤
- Click **"Submit for Approval"** to queue the trades.
- If **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)** is enabled, you will receive an email.
- You can also **"Approve Now"** directly in the dashboard to move to the execution phase.

---

## 🧠 4. AI Insights & Research
Need a second opinion?
- **Chat Assistant**: Ask questions like *"Why is the Energy sector lagging?"* or *"Should I sell reliance?"*. The AI has full context of your portfolio.
- **Health Reports**: Generate deep-dive PDF/Text reports on your portfolio's long-term sustainability.

---

## ⚙️ 5. Configuration (Config)
Manage your strategy without touching code.
- **Agent Settings**: Adjust the AI Model, Risk Threshold, and Global Budget.
- **Investment Universe**: Add or remove tickers from your "watchlist". Define **Target Amounts** and **Weights** to guide the AI's allocation math.

---

## 💡 Best Practices
- **Run Analysis during Market Hours**: For accurate LTP (Last Traded Price).
- **Check the Logic Stream**: Always verify the "AI Rationale" before a large trade.
- **Keep Secrets Secret**: Never share your Dashboard URL if it's exposed to the public internet.
